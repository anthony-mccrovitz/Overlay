### Fix Batter HR Projection (Per-Batter Power)
**What:** `src/data/player_props.py:366-382` projects HR probability using a single per-game lambda (`0.115`, K9-adjusted) shared across every batter in the game. Result: every hitter projects to the exact same HR rate (~0.10), so the model only ever finds UNDER 0.5 HR edges and never identifies a positive YES HR pick.
**Why:** HR rate is one of the most batter-specific stats in baseball (ISO, barrel%, HR/PA, handedness vs SP, ballpark factor). A pooled lambda throws away the entire signal. Right now we're effectively unable to recommend YES HR bets, which kills a category users explicitly ask for.
**Fix:** Pull per-batter HR/PA (rolling 30/season), apply ballpark HR factor, apply handedness split vs opposing SP, then compute `lam = HR_per_PA * expected_PA * park_factor * platoon_factor` and `p_over = 1 - exp(-lam)`. The batter stats are already collected in `src/models/mlb_batter_props.py` (see `batter_hr_per_game` at line 266) — just need to route them through.
**Effort:** M
**Priority:** P1
